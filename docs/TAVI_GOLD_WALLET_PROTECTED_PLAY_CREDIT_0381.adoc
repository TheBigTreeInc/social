= Tavi Core Wallet: Play-only Gold, earned Gold and server-owned checkout
:revnumber: 0.3.81
:revdate: 2026-10-21
:status: source candidate / operator acceptance pending

== Goal and hard invariant

Tavi is a messenger first. Wallet is a bundled, independently enabled first-party extension.
All Gold is virtual, nonredeemable and cannot be exchanged for real money or cash.
The earlier schema-11 Wallet granted the same fungible Gold for both gameplay and shopping.
This is corrected in schema 12. *No balance originating in the free welcome/refill faucet may ever pay a shop item or purchase, directly or via conversion, refunds or another currency.*

Gold remains one displayed currency, but each account/currency tracks two mutually exclusive balances:

* `playOnly`: issued by the free welcome grant and refill; available for operator-priced `entry` offers, never for items, conversions or player transfers.
* `availableForShopping`: earned through reviewed game-service rewards or reversible conversion of shopping-eligible currency; available for items, entries and approved conversions.
* `amount` is the sum of these balances. No endpoint allows the client to specify or change the funding category.

The game uses play-only funds first for a paid `entry`; if insufficient, the remainder is debited from earned funds.
An `entry` can also have zero price. The faucet refills only `playOnly` up to 100 Gold whenever it is below 100. The earned balance is never increased by the faucet, even if the user already has earned Gold.

== Purchase, exchange and provenance rules

`WalletOrder` offers have operator-approved `type` (`entry` or `item`), `currencyId`, merchant and price.
The `purchase` endpoint independently checks the published offer and the player's reviewed quote.
For `item`, the full price must be covered by `availableForShopping`. The policy is server-side, including for direct SDK calls; the UI cannot authorize bypasses.
For `entry`, `restrictedSpent` is stored on the signed journal receipt. On refund, precisely that amount becomes play-only again. Other refunded funds remain shopping-eligible. Schema-11 legacy purchases lacking provenance are refunded conservatively as play-only.

Currency conversion is only allowed from `availableForShopping`. Free Gold therefore cannot become spendable points, coins, or Gold again through round-trip conversion. Reverse conversion preserves the shopping-eligible class because its source must already be eligible.

User clients and sandboxed remote extensions cannot mint Gold. The first-party extension receives only a tokenless host bridge; it never reads Social session credentials.

== Earned rewards (service-only, disabled by default)

A trusted LoopRD service caller, independently authorized with `social:service`, may credit earned funds through `/ecosystem/wallet-reward` using `tavi.wallet-reward/v1` only when its server-side merchant ID is admitted by both:

* `LOOPRD_TAVI_WALLET_MERCHANTS_JSON` (existing merchant enrollment)
* `LOOPRD_TAVI_WALLET_REWARDS_JSON` (new earned-reward permission and limits)

Example **operator configuration only** (never a suggested live default):

[source,json]
----
{
  "example-game": {
    "currencyId": "gold",
    "maxPerEvent": "100",
    "dailyPerPlayer": "500"
  }
}
----

With `LOOPRD_TAVI_WALLET_REWARDS_JSON` absent or invalid, service payouts fail closed.
Each reward requires a recipient account, unique publisher event ID, approved currency and integer amount.
The wallet protects against duplicate/replayed events using a unique `(merchant,eventId)` constraint, enforces a hard maximum event and per-player UTC-day limit, and records a balanced treasury/user journal entry.
A game must separately prove that the participant actually earned the reward; **source tests do not prove a game's rule-side eligibility or service principal enrollment**. No client can invoke the service-only route.

== Schema migration and compatibility

* `11 -> 12` is additive: `WalletRestrictedBalance` and `WalletRewardIssue`, with their indexes.
* **Conservative upgrade:** Every existing positive player currency balance from v11 is seeded as play-only. In v11, free Gold could be converted into another currency; the provenance of preexisting funds is therefore not reliable. Existing funds remain visible and usable for game entry, not shopping or conversion, until a separate audited operator decision establishes an eligible source.
* Historical WalletEntry / WalletTransaction rows, Social messages, accounts and sessions are not rewritten; no balances are deleted or converted into real money.
* If upgrading directly from schema 10, migrations 11 and 12 run in order and the new source grants the initial 100 Gold as play-only.
* Back up production and rehearse both the `10 -> 11 -> 12` and `11 -> 12` paths on isolated copies. Verify that a wallet holding only old free Gold cannot buy shop items or convert.
* Do not deploy or perform an unattended database migration from this source candidate. Approval and live identity/service validation remain mandatory.

== Acceptance matrix

. A new user sees 100 total / 100 play-only / 0 shopping-eligible Gold; shop item and conversion both fail.
. After paying an 80-Gold game entry, refill adds 80 play-only; shopping remains 0.
. A service-authorized earned award of 60 Gold changes balances to 160 total / 100 play-only / 60 shopping-eligible.
. A 25-Gold shop item debits only earned Gold; a refunded item restores eligible Gold.
. A mixed-funding entry debits free Gold first; refund restores each original category without laundering.
. Attempt replay of purchase, refund, refill and earned-reward event IDs; duplicated or conflicting events do not mint or charge a second time.
. Test conversion from free and earned funds, the daily cap, second account isolation, and account/merchant scoping.
. Confirm the real Wallet extension reflects the split at phone, tablet and Windows widths without leaking a token or claiming live game fulfillment.
. Verify ZipRunner workboard IDs and original operator criteria persist; native device and real LoopRD merchant/game acceptance remain open.

== Future work

Cross-realm rewards, user transfers, item ownership, actual Life/Atrium seat redemption and standalone Ledger extraction are separate gated contracts.
Refill Gold may not be turned into shopping eligibility through any of those future integrations. Do not implement speculative fiat, external deposits or cash-out.
